Guides

How to bring your tracks in from a watch, a file or another service, and what to do with them once they are on the map.

  • Open GPX, FIT and TCX files on a map

    See a track file's route on a map of the world, and keep the ones you care about together.

  • Bring in your Garmin activities

    Export an activity from Garmin Connect as GPX, TCX or FIT, or take the file straight off the watch.

  • Bring in your Strava activities

    Export one activity as GPX or its original file, or pick the ones you want from your Strava archive.

  • A Fog of War map of everywhere you have been

    Watch the fog clear wherever your tracks go, and find the streets, parks and trails close to home you have never been to.
