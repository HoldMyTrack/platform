Every place you have ever run, ridden or walked — on one map.

Your watch or fitness app shows one workout at a time, and the old ones get lost when you switch apps or devices. HoldMyTrack keeps the trips worth remembering on one map, so you can see the places you know by heart and the streets, parks and trails you've never set foot on. It's free for everyone, with no paid tiers, and your data is never sold.

How it works

  1. Bring what you already recorded

    From Strava, Garmin, any watch or bike computer, or {{if eq .App "ios"}}your iPhone{{else}}your Android phone{{end}}: pick the trips worth keeping. Nothing new to record.

    Guides: Strava · Garmin{{if not .InApp}} · Android app{{end}}

  2. See it all on one map

    Every track is drawn together. Switch to Fog of War, which covers the map and clears it wherever you have been, or to the heatmap, which glows where you go most.

  3. Go somewhere new

    The fog shows what is left. Pick a street, a park or a trail you have never been to, go, and watch it clear next time you upload.

What it does

  • One map, three views: Every track at once; Fog of War, which clears wherever you've ever been; and a heatmap of the last year.
  • Somewhere new, close to home: The fog shows the nearby streets, parks and trails you haven't been to yet.
  • Trails and bike paths: Trails and dirt tracks are always on the map, and bike paths, shared paths and mountain-bike trails from OpenStreetMap can be turned on in Layers. They draw over the fog, so you can see which unexplored one to try next.
  • Stories: Group the activities of a multi-day hike or a holiday into one trip, with its own map, totals and photos.
  • Photos on the route: Add your pictures to an activity and each one sits on the route where it was taken.
  • Spots: Playgrounds, viewpoints, waterfalls and historic sites from OpenStreetMap. Spend 30 seconds in one with capture mode on in the Android app, and it stays marked on your map.
  • Image export: Share a high-resolution picture of any view; your map itself is never public.
  • Fix a track without losing it: Chop a recording down to the stretch you want, cut out a detour, move or delete stray GPS points, or split one recording into two activities. The original is never changed, so Reset brings the track back as recorded.
  • Private by default: Nobody else sees your map, and Private locations hide where tracks start and end near home or work.
  • Your years at a glance: A square for every day you got out, with weekly or monthly distances.

What it isn't

  • Not a replacement for your watch or tracking app. Keep recording the way you already do; HoldMyTrack starts where the recording ends.
  • Not a coach or a health app. There are no training plans, readiness scores or HR zones, and no heart rate at all. It is about where you went, not how your body did.
  • Not just for athletes. Anyone can use it in their own way, from kids to retirees: a daily dog walk counts as much as a marathon.
  • Not a social network. No feed, followers, kudos or leaderboards. Your map is yours alone.

Free and open

  • 100% free: No subscriptions and no paid features.
  • Your data isn't locked in: Download all of it, your original files included and every track as GPX. It is never sold.
  • No tracking: No analytics services or trackers. The site only counts visitors per day, without following anyone (how).
  • Yours to host: Run it on your own server with the deployment guide.
  • Open source: MIT-licensed, so you can look under the hood.

The site uses cookies only to keep you signed in and to remember the language you picked, plus a short-lived one while you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple.
